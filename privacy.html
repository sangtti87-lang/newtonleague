Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy describes how the "Newton League" publishing tool (the "App") handles data. The App is an internal, single-owner tool used solely to publish videos and captions to the YouTube channel @newtonleague, which is owned and operated by the same person who operates the App.

What the App does

  • Uploads videos, thumbnails and caption tracks to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads basic metadata of the owner's own channel and videos (titles, privacy status) to schedule and verify uploads.

Google user data

The App uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API with the scope youtube.force-ssl. Access is granted only by the channel owner for the owner's own account. The App does not access, collect, store or share any data belonging to other users or viewers.

OAuth tokens are stored on a private server controlled by the owner and are used exclusively to perform the actions above. Tokens are never shared with third parties.

Data we do not collect

The App has no public users. It does not collect personal information from viewers, does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and does not sell or transfer data to anyone.

Limited Use disclosure

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the contact link on the YouTube channel Newton League.
